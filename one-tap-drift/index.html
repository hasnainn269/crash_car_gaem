HOW TO PLAY

Survive the endless curves and climb the leaderboard.

  • 1 Auto-Drive: Your sports car accelerates and tracks the asphalt automatically. No manual steering needed.
  • 2 One-Tap Drift: Watch the drift timing meter as you approach each curve. Tap when the needle enters the blue sweet spot!
  • 3 Perfect Timing: Perfect drifts award +100 pts, bonus coins, and build up your Combo Multiplier up to 8x!
  • 4 Don't Miss: Missing the apex deadline causes the car to violently skid off-road and wreck!
  • 5 Collect Coins: Pick up gold coins along the road to unlock custom race cars in the Garage.
